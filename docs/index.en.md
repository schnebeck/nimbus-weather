# Nimbus – weather for Germany and Europe

Nimbus shows the weather with small animations of the sky: readings of the nearest weather
station, the forecast of the finest model for the place, the rain radar of DWD and KNMI, pollen,
water levels and bathing waters. No ads, no account, no Google services – free software (GPL-3.0).
