<p>Nimbus reflects the current weather with small matching background animations – drifting clouds, rain, snow or a starry sky. The data comes from Europe's weather services – readings from the nearest weather station, the forecast of the finest model for the place or of a model of your choice and the rain radar of the DWD and the KNMI. No ads, no account, no Google services.</p>

<p><b>Weather</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Current conditions measured at the nearest weather station (DWD, GeoSphere Austria, MeteoSwiss, DMI, else an airport) – only at the place's height; the sky follows the measured sunshine</li>
<li>DWD weather alerts</li>
<li>Short text outlook for the next hours and precipitation in 15-minute steps</li>
<li>Hourly and 10-day forecast with chance of precipitation</li>
<li>Meteogram for every day: temperature, precipitation, sunshine and wind per hour, with a slider for the exact values</li>
<li>Look back: swipe right to compare what was measured with what was forecast for today, yesterday and the day before – with the rain radar of the whole day</li>
<li>Bathing waters nearby and favourites: EU classification, sea temperature, water temperature and blue-green algae notices in Berlin and Schleswig-Holstein</li>
<li>Feels like, UV, wind, humidity and dew point, visibility, air pressure</li>
<li>Sun course with golden and blue hour, moon phase and moon times</li>
<li>Air quality, pollen forecast (DWD index and composition, choose the types you care about), citizen sensor network, comparison of eight weather models</li>
<li>Tides at the coast and on tidal rivers: next high and low waters with a tide curve, calculated from gauge readings</li>
<li>Water levels of the rivers nearby, one gauge per river – measured values from the federal waterways and from Lower Saxony, North Rhine-Westphalia, Saxony and Hesse, elsewhere the flood classification of the states; plus their flood alerts</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Rain radar</b></p>
<ul>
<li>DWD radar with a 2-hour nowcast for Germany, KNMI radar for the Netherlands, MET Norway's radar for Norway, Sweden, Finland and Denmark, RainViewer for the rest of Europe</li>
<li>One colour scale for rain and for snow – decided per pixel by the temperature</li>
<li>Temperature and wind layers, satellite and warning map, look back up to 24 hours</li>
<li>On Wi-Fi the radar loop of your location is kept up to date in the background, so it opens instantly</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Details</b></p>
<ul>
<li>An ⓘ on every card explains the terms</li>
<li>English and German, units follow your country (°C/°F, km/h, mph, m/s, knots, Beaufort, mm/inch)</li>
<li>Forecast model: automatically the finest for each place and time, or DWD ICON, ECMWF, Météo-France and regional models such as MET Nordic (1 km) or KNMI Harmonie (2 km) – a model of its own for each place too</li>
<li>Battery friendly: the animation slows down when the phone is left alone and stops in battery saver mode</li>
</ul>
